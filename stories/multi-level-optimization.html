Pricing for more than sell-through

Impact Analytics · 2023

Multi-level optimization let retailers plan discounts against revenue, margin and units, and it ran department-wide.

7enterprise retail clients

One number wasn’t enough

Our pricing tool helped retailers plan discounts, and it judged everything on one thing: sell-through as a percentage of the inventory.

Retailers care about more than that. Revenue, profit margin and units sold can matter more than the sell-through percentage, depending on the business.

I led the integration of multi-level optimization (MLO) so a client could choose what to optimize for: revenue, margin, margin % or units.

Department-wide, in one go

MLO adds a lot of computation. Running it the old way, one person running an experiment on a small subset, over and over, wasn’t going to hold up.

So we changed the product itself. Simulations ran department-wide, as bulk transformations across all the data at once, instead of picking the same small subset again and again. Less compute and less time.

Holding the runtime

I redesigned the data pipeline to absorb the extra load. Runtime held at about 3 minutes with no SLA breaches, and 7 enterprise retail clients adopted it. Later, as an engineer, I shipped goal-based promotion planning on top: set a revenue, margin or unit target, and the engine finds the discount depth that hits it from historical sales response.
