Why has our revenue history stopped moving?

Asked at Hiver, 2026

A pipeline bug had silently frozen 15 months of revenue history, for every customer. Nobody had noticed because the numbers still looked like numbers.

I defined the fix, then checked the result against billing one day at a time, until reported revenue and invoices agreed.

The daily gap is now 0.04%. On an ordinary scale it doesn't show up. You have to zoom in twice to find it.

Where are our new users coming from?

Asked at HackerRank, 2025

Leadership reporting showed acquisition trends, but 30% of new users had no known source. A bug was hiding almost a third of the story.

I rebuilt how sources are classified: parsing UTM parameters, and mapping IP addresses to countries in a GDPR-compliant way.

Unknown-source users went from 30% to zero. Every new user now has a source leadership can act on.

How big is our audience, really?

Asked at HackerRank, 2025

The number everyone quoted was 27.4M registered developers. Each dot here is 100,000 of them.

The useful number was the people still showing up: 3.5M active developers, about one in eight.

Cut by region and segment, that engagement and retention picture went into H1 2026 planning and positioning.

Is reported revenue too high?

Asked at Hiver, 2026

Reported revenue looked healthy. Too healthy, once you set it against the true figure, here fixed at 100.

Duplicate records were inflating it by 4.5%. Small enough to pass a glance, large enough to change a forecast.

I removed them at the source, so every dashboard built on top now starts from the true number.
