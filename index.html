    Dear hiring team,

    I help B2B SaaS teams make product decisions they can defend with data. I'm a senior product analyst at Hiver, and over six years at Mu Sigma, Impact Analytics, HackerRank and Hiver I have worked across the whole analytics stack: instrumentation, data modelling, dashboards, and the meeting where the decision actually gets made.

    My path is a little unusual for an analyst. I started in data science, spent two years building a pricing optimization engine as an engineer, and then moved into product analytics. So I don't stop at the dashboard.

    I can design the tracking plan, build the pipeline and the model, and then sit with a PM to work out what we should do next. I work in SQL, Python, Amplitude, Metabase, Superset, Redshift, Trino and FastAPI.

    The part of the job I like most is when a stakeholder asks a question, the data gives an answer that surprises them, and then we do something with it. I believe the best analysts make non-data people feel like data people.

    I'm looking for a senior or lead product analyst role in B2B SaaS, where analytics sits close to the product and the people building it.
